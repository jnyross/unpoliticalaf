# Unpolitical phone test: information and consent

Please read this before you start. The moderator will answer any questions.

## What this is

Unpolitical is a website that explains where UK public money goes, who pays for it,
and how everyday life has changed. We want to know whether people who don't follow
politics closely can understand it on a phone.

**We are testing the website, not you.** There are no trick questions about your views,
and nobody will see how you did except the people running the test.

## What you will do

- Answer 12 multiple-choice questions using the website on your own phone.
- You have 8 minutes. The moderator will time you and will not help with answers.
- It takes about 15 minutes in all, including this note.

## What we record

- Your answers, how long you took, and the make of phone and browser you used.
- Your age band and whether you live in the UK, to check you can take part.
- A participant code, such as P3. **We do not write your name on your answers.**

We do not record your screen, your voice or your political views.

## How we use it

- Your answers are added up with four other people's to see whether the website
  explains things clearly enough. The total and anonymous per-person scores may be
  published in the project's records (for example "P3: 10 of 12").
- Your signed consent, if on paper, is kept separately from your answers and is
  destroyed once the results are recorded.

## Your choices

- Taking part is voluntary. You can stop at any time without giving a reason.
- You can ask for your answers to be removed until the results are published.
