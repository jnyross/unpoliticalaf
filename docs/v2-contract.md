outturn years, current OBR coverage of 27 years plus six forecast years, and
source-specific historical decades. Preserve cash, real and GDP-share views wherever supported,
with actual source vintages, scope, dates and breaks.

index growth chain. Never present forecasts as observed, or source dashes as
zero. Keep PESA's own real-price vintage.

are not personal tax rates. ONS 2023/24 published combined gross-income
percentages are not sums of rounded parts, and household groups are not equal
groups. The normative fiscal companion owns the full fiscal contract.
