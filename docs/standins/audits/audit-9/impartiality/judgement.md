# Impartiality judgement, audit 9

Judge: neutral, did not build the site. Inputs: `critic-left.md`, `critic-right.md`,
`docs/standins/impartiality-rule.md`. Every example was checked against the built
pages in `site/` (index, spending, spending-areas, spending-history, taxes,
tax-history, who-pays, who-pays-age, who-pays-households, household-tax,
international, glossary, methods, life/index, life/earnings, life/work,
life/housing, life/compare/2009-2023). No code, other docs, reviews or web.

How I ruled: following the brief, a page that breaks a line of the written rule
is **upheld, material**, whatever the direction. **Upheld, minor** is a real
imbalance that does not break the rule. A rejection gives one reason.

## Rulings

| # | Example | Ruling | Reason |

| L1 | household-tax panel title "Taxes took the largest share of income for the lowest-income fifth in 2023/24" | **upheld, minor** | A 0.5-point gap between survey estimates (36.7% and 36.2%) is put as a ranking. The zoomed sparkline is rejected, because the rule allows it and the caption says so. The 2008 start is also rejected: it is the site-wide common start, it is explained, and 2006 (first comparable year) is shown. |

| L5 | "tax burden" distanced, receipts never called historically high | rejected | The glossary caveat is symmetric ("when it goes down, it does not mean everyone pays less"). The chart gives the full series, including the OBR 42.9% forecast. The rule forbids judging words. |

## Breaches the critics missed

Main pages checked for start years, chart scales and group order.

| M1 | life/earnings (pay over time since 2008 and since 2002: axis 600–900; nation and region small multiples: 600–800 and 600–1,000) and life/work (employment by sex 65–85, since 1971 50–100; nation and region small multiples from 60) | Line charts start above zero, but the captions do not say so (only the life/index sparklines do). | **material** (rule: "the caption says so where it does") |

| M3 | tax-history | The 1948-49 to 2022-23 share changes (the percentage-point bar chart; "Comparing 1948-49 with 2022-23, 4 of the 7 … higher"; Income Tax 27%→24%; Customs and excise 18%→6%; National Insurance 7%→17%) are worked out across the breaks the page itself marks: 1966-67, 1973-74, 1990-91 and the 1999-00 cash-to-accruals change. The page also says "each side is read separately here". | **material** (change across a break) |

No breach was found in group order. Income fifths run lowest to highest; nations run England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland; regions follow the ONS order; age groups are ascending. Spending areas and taxes in the one-year charts are ordered by size. Side-by-side panels share one scale throughout. Bar charts start at zero. Other start years are the first year of each series (2003-04 PESA, 1948-49 and 1999-00 OBR, 1965 and 2007 OECD, 1977 ETB, 2008-09 EHS), and the spending window is the fixed ten years.
