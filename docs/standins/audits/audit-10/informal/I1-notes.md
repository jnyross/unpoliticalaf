# I1 notes (19, café in Leeds, phone)

## What I found out
- Where most money goes: the biggest chunk is pensions, benefits and social care, £33 out of every £100 spent on services. Health is £21, then general public services £13. Debt interest is £11 of every £100. Total is £1,360bn this year, which is a number I can't picture.
- Tax: on average 33.6% of household income goes on tax (about a third), 22.9% direct like Income Tax and 10.7% indirect like VAT. Poorest fifth pay 36.7%, richest 36.2%, so it's about the same for everyone, just different taxes. That surprised me.
- Change: for housing, more people rent privately now (14% in 2008-09, 19% in 2024-25), fewer buy with a mortgage. Average private rent is £250 a week. For work, 75.3% of 16 to 64s are in work, and the gap between men and women has shrunk.
