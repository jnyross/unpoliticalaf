## What I found out
- Where the money goes: biggest bit is pensions, benefits and social care, £33 of every £100, then health £21. Total £1,360bn.
- Tax on a household: 33.6% of gross income on average (2023/24). Lowest-income fifth pays 36.7%, highest 36.2%. Odd, the poor pay about the same.
- Housing since 1981: owning outright went up to 35.5%, renting privately went up to 18.9%, council renting came down to 16.3%. 65% own their home now.
