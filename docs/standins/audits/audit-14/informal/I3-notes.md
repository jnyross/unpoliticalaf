# I3 notes (P8, taxi driver, 58, Manchester)

## What I found out
- Where the money goes: biggest bit is pensions, benefits and social care, 33 quid in every 100. Health 21, general services 13.
- Tax on a household: 33.6% of gross income on average (2023/24). Poorest fifth pay 36.7%, richest 36.2%. Surprised me.
- Pay since I was young: only found "about the same after inflation, 2021 to 2025" (761 to 767 a week). Charts go back to 2002 and housing to 1981, but nothing said plainly what changed since the 1980s.
