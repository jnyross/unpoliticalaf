`critic-right.md` and `docs/standins/impartiality-rule.md`. Every example was
checked against the built pages in `site/` (converted to text, plus a check of
the bar-width markup). I did not read the scorecard, the answer key or any
other audit folder.

## Own check: start years, scales and order

- **Start years.** Spending over time 2003-04 to 2025-26 and receipts 1948-49 to
  2025-26 are first-to-latest; spending by area uses the fixed ten years
  2015-16 to 2025-26; earnings, housing, household-tax, tax-history and
  spending-history all split at their series breaks and do not compute changes
  across them. The international and borrowing time charts run first year
  (1990 or 2007) to latest. No start year chosen for the result was found.
- **Scales.** Bar widths are proportional to value from zero on every bar list
  checked (index, spending, taxes, tax-history, borrowing; diverging bars for
  changes). Every line chart starting above zero says so in its caption.
  Small-multiple panels state "All panels use the same scale". Cash,
  after-inflation and share-of-GDP views are labelled.
- **Order.** Income fifths, age bands and English regions are in official order;
  spending areas and taxes are by size; country charts are rankings and say so
  (rank stated, or "second lowest", "5th of 6").
- **Wording.** No party, government or politician is named as responsible for a
  change; explanations are attributed quotes, apart from L10.
