# I1 notes (P5, 27, warehouse worker, Birmingham, English second language)

I used 16 of 40 actions.

## What I found out

- Where the money goes: the UK spent £1,360bn in 2025-26. Out of every £100 spent on services, £33 goes on pensions and benefits (social protection) and £21 on health. Education is £10 and defence is £5.
- Tax on household income: 33.6% of income goes on tax on average in 2023/24. 22.9% is direct tax (Income Tax) and 10.7% is indirect tax (VAT). The poorest fifth pay 36.7% and the richest fifth 36.2%, which surprised me. It says this is an average, not my own tax rate.
- Housing, since I was young: in 2024-25 renting privately cost £250 a week on average, and 19% of households rent privately, up from 14% in 2008-09. Social rent is £129 and a mortgage is £242. After inflation, private rent was £236 in 2008-09, so it has gone up a little. I did not open the pay, class size or holiday pages.
