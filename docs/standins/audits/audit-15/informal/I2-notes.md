# I2 notes (P1: 19, café worker, Leeds, phone only)

## What I found out
- Where the money goes: the UK public sector spent £1,360bn in 2025-26. Out of every £100 spent on services, £33 goes on social protection (pensions, benefits, social care) and £21 on health. So mostly pensions and benefits, then the NHS. (Spending page.)
- Tax: on average 33.6% of household income goes on tax (22.9% direct like Income Tax, 10.7% VAT and the like). Poorest and richest fifth are about the same (36.7% vs 36.2%), which surprised me. (Tax and income page.)
- Change since I was young: I looked at housing and holidays. Renting privately went from 14% of households in 2008-09 to 19% in 2024-25, and the average private rent is £250 a week. Holiday visits abroad were 58.4 million in 2025, about 86 per 100 people, mostly Spain. I did not work out how much my own rent compares to that.
