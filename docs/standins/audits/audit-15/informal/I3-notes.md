# I3 (P8, 58, taxi driver, Manchester) - notes

## What I found out
- Where the money goes: £1,360bn spent in 2025-26. Biggest bit is pensions, benefits and social care, £33 of every £100. Health is £21. Found it on the home page and the Spending page.
- Tax on a household: 33.6% of income on average (2023/24). Roughly 23% direct (income tax) and 11% VAT-type taxes. Poorest fifth and richest fifth pay about the same share, 36.7% vs 36.2%. That surprised me.
- Pay since I was young: after inflation, pay in 2025 is about the same as 2021 (£761 to £767 a week). Gap between men and women is narrowing. But the chart only goes back to 2002, and nothing about when I was 20 (1980s).
