# I2 notes (41, parent of two, Belfast, part-time admin, reads in short gaps)

Used 12 of 40 actions, phone, home page then the housing page.

## What I found out, in my words

- Where most money goes: out of every £100 spent on services, £33 goes on social protection (pensions, benefits, social care), £21 on health and £13 on "general public services" which is mostly debt interest. Total is £1,360bn for 2025-26. Home page.
- Tax as a share of household income: 33.6% on average, about a third. Lowest fifth 36.7%, middle 30.2%, highest 36.2%. It says that is an average, not my tax rate. Home page.
- Housing since I was young: 65% of households in England own their home (2024-25). Renting privately has gone from 14% in 2008-09 to 19%. Average private rent £250 a week, mortgage £242, social rent £129. After inflation, rents and mortgage payments went up and down rather than just climbing. Housing page. It is England only, so not quite my Belfast.
