# I2 notes (nurse, Cardiff, phone)

## What I found out, in my words
- Where most money goes: out of every 100 pounds the government spends on services, about 33 goes on social protection (pensions, benefits, social care). Health is 21, and 13 is "general public services" which mostly seems to be interest on debt. Total spent: 1,360bn in 2025-26. (Home page.)
- Tax on household income: on average 33.6% of gross household income goes on tax (2023/24). The poorest fifth is 36.7%, the richest 36.2%, the middle about 30%. So roughly a third. (Home page.)
- How work has changed: 75.1% of people 16 to 64 were in work in April to June 2026. The gap between men's and women's employment shrank from 39 points in 1971 to 6 points in 2025. Manufacturing fell from 24.9% of jobs in 1978 to 6.7% in 2026. (Work page.) I also glanced at Housing: fewer people rent from the council, more rent privately, but I did not read it through.
