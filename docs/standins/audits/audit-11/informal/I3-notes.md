# I3 notes (taxi driver, 58, Manchester; phone, 23 of 40 actions used)

## What I found out, in my words

- Where the money goes: biggest lump is pensions and benefits, £33 out of every £100. Health is next at £21. Debt interest is £11. (Home page, then Spending page.)
- Tax: on average a third of what a household earns goes on tax, 33.6%. Poorest fifth pay 36.7%, richest 36.2%, middle 30.2%. So the middle pays least, which surprised me. (Home page.)
- Housing since I was young: renting privately has gone up (14% in 2008-09 to 19% now), and fewer are buying with a mortgage. 65% own their home now. (Housing page.) I could not see anything from when I was young, as it only goes back to 2008.
