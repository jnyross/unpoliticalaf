## Breaches the critics missed

I checked start years, chart scales and group order on the main pages: index, spending, spending-areas, spending-history, taxes, tax-history, who-pays, household-tax, borrowing, international and the life pages.

- **Start years.** Every other comparison uses the first and latest year of its series, or splits at a stated series break: housing at 2008-09, holidays at 2009 and 2019, earnings at 2011 and 2021, and the international spending series from 2007, its first year. The 2010-11 to 2019-20 sentence (R1) is the only window outside the rule.
- **Scales.** Bar and line axes in the text versions start at 0. The earnings line chart starts above zero and its caption says so. Panels compared side by side (spending by area, G7 debt) say "All panels use the same scale".
- **Order.** Income fifths are in official order (lowest to highest). Spending areas and taxes are by size. The G7 bars are ranked, and the text says so ("ranked 5th of 6, where 1st is the highest").

I found no further breaches.
