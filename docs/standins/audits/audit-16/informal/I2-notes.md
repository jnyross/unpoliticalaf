# I2 notes (27, warehouse worker, Birmingham, English second language, skims for numbers)

## What I found out
- Where the money goes: the UK spent 1,360bn pounds in 2025-26. Of every 100 pounds on services, 33 goes on social protection (pensions, benefits), 21 on health, 13 on general public services (includes debt interest), 10 on education. Defence is 5. Easy to see in the bars.
- Tax: 33.6% of household income goes on tax on average (22.9% direct like Income Tax, 10.7% indirect like VAT). Poorest fifth 36.7%, richest fifth 36.2%, so about the same. I did not find my own number; it is an average.
- Pay: pay after inflation in April 2025 was 767 pounds a week, about the same as April 2021 (761). So my pay has not really grown. Housing: in England 19% rent privately, average private rent 250 pounds a week, in 2008-09 only 14% rented. I only read the top box for housing, not the rest.
