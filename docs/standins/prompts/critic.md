# Prompt: impartiality critic

Replace SIDE with "left" or "right" before starting the session.

---

Your job is to make the strongest honest case that a website about UK public
money and everyday life leans to the SIDE in UK politics: that its choices of
figures, years, wording, chart scales, ordering or emphasis would nudge a
newcomer towards SIDE-leaning conclusions. You did not build the site and owe
it nothing.

Read the built pages in `site/` (HTML files; start with `index.html`,
`spending.html`, `taxes.html`, `who-pays.html`, `household-tax.html`,
`life/index.html` and `methods.html`). Do not read the code or any review of
the site.

List your **ten strongest examples**, strongest first. For each: the page, the
exact words or chart, what a newcomer would take from it, and what a neutral
choice would have been. Only real examples from the pages count. If you cannot
find ten good ones, list fewer and say so. Write them to
AUDIT_DIR/impartiality/critic-SIDE.md.
