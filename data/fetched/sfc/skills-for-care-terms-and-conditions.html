1. Membership application

Membership of Skills for Care is open to registered managers in adult social care working in England.

Membership is personal to an individual and cannot be ‘lent’, sold or transferred to another individual. All benefits, offers and services are only to be used by the named individual member.

Following purchase, Skills for Care will primarily communicate with the member; regardless of whether membership was bought on their behalf or otherwise.

2. Payment

Membership is fixed for a term of 12 months unless otherwise specifically stated and starts on the day your payment is received. You will receive an email to confirm receipt of payment.

Payment may be made by card using our online registration portal. Alternatively, payment can be made by cheque or you can be invoiced by Skills for Care.

When making payment you will be asked to confirm that either you are the card holder or that you have authority from the card holder to make the transaction.  

3. Personal details

You are responsible for ensuring that all information provided by you to us is true and accurate at the point of applying.

In the event of a change of circumstances such as moving jobs, change of contact details or any other change that will affect your profile, you must update your profile through your account on the Skills for Care website, by informing the membership team by email (membership@skillsforcare.org.uk).

Your personal data includes all the information we hold that identifies you or is about you, for example, your name, email address, postal address, date of birth, location data and in some cases opinions that we document about you.

Skills for Care is the data controller of the personal data you provide. We have appointed Mark Hyslop as Data Protection Officer who will have day to day responsibility for ensuring we comply with the Data Protection Legislation and dealing with any requests we receive from individuals exercising their rights under the Data Protection Legislation.

The information which you give when completing your application form for membership will be used in accordance with the Data Protection Legislation and we will process it for the following purposes on the grounds of our legitimate interests in promoting and maintaining the benefits of the membership scheme:

  • to enable us to create an electronic record of your application for membership
  • to enable the application for membership to be processed
  • to enable us to contact you with important information regarding your membership (including renewal notifications)
  • to enable us to share membership benefits with you (including, though not limited to, your monthly newsletter, opportunities to test specific products, details of discounted products and services)
  • to enable us to compile statistics, provided that no statistical information that identifies you as an individual would be published without your prior consent

You can opt out of all marketing communications from the membership team if you wish by contacting our information team email membership@skillsforcare.org.uk.

The information that we hold about you will be kept securely and will be kept for up to six years following the end of your membership. Please note that if your membership ends, we will still hold information about you if you have subscribed to other Skills for Care services, please refer to the main Skills for Care privacy policy for further information about how we handle your data. After your membership ends we may contact you to advise you that your membership has expired and to invite you to re-subscribe to membership, as detailed below under “Renewal”. You will not receive any further communications from the membership team after this.

We may transfer your personal data to third parties for the sole purpose of helping us fulfil our obligations and exercise our rights. We will not transfer your personal data to any third party for marketing purposes. We will not transfer your personal data outside of the EU.

  • right to restrict processing
  • data portability
  • right to object to us processing your personal data
  • right to object to automated decision making (note that we do not carry out any automated decision making using your personal data)

If you have any concerns about the way your data is being used, please email membership@skillsforcare.org.uk. If you think we have processed your personal data unlawfully or that we have not complied with GDPR, you can also report your concerns to the supervisory authority in your jurisdiction. The supervisory authority in the UK is the Information Commissioner’s Office (“ICO”). You can call the ICO on 0303 123 1113 or get in touch via other means, as set out on the ICO website.

Membership is for the term of 12 months unless otherwise specifically stated. Membership will automatically expire after this time unless you have renewed your membership or you have set-up a subscription via recurring payment.

You will receive renewal reminders from Skills for Care, which include full details of how to renew. If your membership lapses, you will not be able to access the members area of the website or your member benefits until the membership is renewed.

If your membership lapses you will be contacted once by Skills for Care, three weeks after your membership expires, with an invitation to renew your membership. After this time you will receive no further correspondence from the membership team.

Where a member has opted into paying for membership via an annual subscription:

  • Skills for Care will take payment via a recurring payment once every 12 months; read about recurring payments.  
  • Skills for Care will not hold your card details; we use the PCI compliant payment gateway provided by Stripe – your card details will be held by Stripe.
  • Skills for Care make use of the Smarter Saved Cards function provided by Stripe.   • You will receive a notification that payment is due 28 days before we attempt to take payment; you will be notified of any change in the price of membership at the same time. If at this point you do not wish to continue your membership you have the option of cancelling your subscription.
  • Your membership will automatically renew once we take payment – when payment is taken you shall be deemed to have accepted the terms and conditions which apply to the coming year.
  • If we are unable to take payment you will be notified via email; we will attempt to take payment again at three, five and seven days after your membership expires. If we are still unable to take payment your membership will lapse.
  • You can cancel your subscription at any point via your ‘Manage my subscription’ page on the Skills for Care portal or by contacting your bank.

8. Cancellation

Your membership services commence immediately your payment is received.

To cancel your membership you'll need to notify us by email, stating a reason, which will be recorded for internal use only.

A 14-day cooling off period applies to all membership purchases and renewals. The 14-day cooling off period starts from the date you join or renew. If you decide to cancel your membership during this time, you must:

  • write to Skills for Care to let us know
  • return the printed membership benefit sent to you when you last purchased or renewed membership; Skills for Care will not issue a refund before goods are returned.

If you wish to cancel your membership after the 14 day cooling off period, you will not be refunded.

If this is proved to be the case, Skills for Care reserves the right to immediately cancel your membership without refunding any part of your membership fee.
