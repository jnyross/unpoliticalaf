    Correction

     

    An error was identified in the calculation of the estimated eligible two year old population, and the resulting uptake rate. The national uptake rate for eligible two year olds was incorrectly stated as 59%. The correct figure is 57%. This error occurred due to human error. The figures within this document have not been amended. However, corrected uptake rates at both national and local authority level will be available from December 9 2025 as part of the revised time series published on the early learning and childcare webpage.

    Pupils

    • 702,428 pupils – down 3,100 from 2023
    • 379,354 primary – down 5,371 from 2023
    • 315,072 Secondary – up 2,011 from 2023
    • 8,002 Special – up 260 from 2023
    • The pupil teacher ratio increased to 13.3
    • The average primary class size increased from 23.2 in 2023 to 23.3 in 2024.

    Teachers

    • 53,412 Teachers – down 621 from 2023
    • 24,468 Primary – down 629 from 2023
    • 24,988 Secondary – down 61 from 2023
    • 2,138 Special – up 62 from 2023
    • 1,140 Centrally employed – up 29 from 2023
    • 679 ELC – down 23 from 2023
    • The percentage of Teacher Induction Scheme probationers teaching in publicly funded schools the following year decreased from 71% in 2023 to 65% in 2024.

    Early learning and Childcare

    • 90,321 registrations, down 1,861 from 2023
    • 95% uptake for age three and four - down from 97% in 2023
    • 4,672 teachers and graduate staff - up 172 from 2023

    Attendance

    • The attendance rate was 90.3%, an increase from 90.2% in 2022/23
