Evidence before figures

Before publishing fiscal data, we will:

  • Link each figure to an official source and identify its year, UK coverage, unit and publication version.
  • Label actual results, estimates and forecasts separately.
  • Check that compatible categories add up to their published totals, and explain rounding, exclusions and any remaining differences.
  • Distinguish cash amounts from inflation-adjusted comparisons, with the method and base year stated.
  • Provide readable text and tables, not information available only in charts or colour.
  • Require independent data and usability review before calling the first usable product complete.

The fiscal year and datasets have not yet been selected. This baseline does not claim that data checks, full accessibility acceptance or factory activation have been completed.

How to check a figure

When figures are published here, use these steps to check them:

  • Follow the official source link to the named publication and table. Check the publication version and the row or column behind the figure.
  • Check the fiscal year, UK coverage and unit, including the scale used. Compare figures only when their periods and definitions match.
  • Read the status label: an actual result, an estimate or a forecast. A forecast describes an expected outcome, not an observed result.
  • Read the reconciliation notes explaining how compatible categories add up to the published total. Check rounding, exclusions and any remaining differences; rounded parts may not add up exactly.
