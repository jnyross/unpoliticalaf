Each annual anchor names the academic year ending in that year. The census is in January. The third-Thursday rule comes from the method page; it is not a separately reported date for each row. All eleven years use this current release. January 2021 is present. No older release is joined to it.

The one-decimal means are pupils divided by each row’s own classes. Changes subtract those unrounded means, then round once. We use decimal arithmetic with 50-digit precision and round halves up. Do not subtract rounded means to check a change.

Some published totals differ from their three size bands. These are total minus band sum. We keep each published total as the share’s whole. We do not force band shares to sum to 100 or assign a cause.
